# Project Brief

## 1. The Question

Which communities in Bosso Local Government Area, Niger State, are more than 5 km from the nearest health facility?


## 2. Why It Matters

Access to healthcare is important for community wellbeing and emergency response. Identifying communities that are located more than 5 km from health facilities can help planners and decision makers identify underserved areas and improve healthcare service delivery.


## 3. Data Needed

- Bosso LGA boundary
- Ward boundaries
- Health facility locations
- Settlement locations
- Road network
- Population data (optional)


## 4. Data Sources

### 1. LGA Boundaries
Source: GRID3 Nigeria Operational LGA Boundaries  
Link: https://data.grid3.org/

### 2. Ward Boundaries
Source: GRID3 Nigeria Operational Wards  
Link: https://data.grid3.org/

### 3. Health Facilities
Source: GRID3 Nigeria Health Facilities  
Link: https://data.grid3.org/

### 4. Road Data
Source: OpenStreetMap  
Link: https://www.openstreetmap.org/

## 5. What I Will Build

I will create a GIS map showing communities in Bosso LGA that are more than 5 km from the nearest health facility. The final output may include an interactive map or dashboard that can support healthcare planning and decision making.
