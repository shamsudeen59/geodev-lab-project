# Project Brief

## 1. The Question

Which communities in Bosso Local Government Area, Niger State, are more than 5 km from the nearest health facility?


## 2. Why It Matters

Access to healthcare is important for community wellbeing and emergency response. Identifying communities that are located more than 5 km from health facilities can help planners and decision makers identify underserved areas and improve healthcare service delivery.


## 3. Data Needed

- Bosso LGA boundary
- Ward boundaries
- Health facility locations
- Road network  (QuickOSM plugin)
